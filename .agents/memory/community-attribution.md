name: Community tattoo attribution
description: Product intent behind connecting client tattoos to artists and studios
---

Client-to-artist/studio attribution is a core product feature, not merely a mention or an approval workflow. The user wants a tattoo community where people share tattoos, connect them to artists and shops, and help professionals promote their work.

**Why:** The user described this community and discovery model as "One of the core reasons i'm starting tis app".

**How to apply:** Preserve open tagging of artist/studio accounts by any community member. Keep attribution visible on the original post. Profile curation is separate from authorship and discovery: hiding attributed client work from a professional's profile must neither change the original post nor override its visibility.
